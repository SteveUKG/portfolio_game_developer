FEAR STATE

A first-person psychological horror game set inside an Indian corporate office — where finishing the workday isn't the only thing trying to break you.

Engine — Unreal Engine 5.8 Perspective — First person Platform — PC / Steam
The Premise

An ordinary office. An escalating wrongness.

You explore the office, work through tasks at a monitor and terminal, and interact with coworkers — including Riya and a manager — as the routine of the workday gradually gives way to horror events. Every door, drawer, and desk is built on the same interaction system, so the office feels consistent and explorable rather than scripted.
